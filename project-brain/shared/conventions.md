# Conventions

- Dates: YYYY-MM-DD.
- IDs are issued once, in order, never renumbered. Retired items are marked VOID.
- Every figure is tagged: [user], [owner:<role>] or [calc: <formula>]. Untagged figures are withheld.
- Unknowns are written "unknown", with the role that can supply them named.
- Gates (G1–G4) need the exact token `[continue]` in reply to a WAITING line.
- Edits to baselines, past reports and logged decisions are made by CORRECTION entries, not in place.
- Preferences (style, tone) live here, not in rules. Rules need a testable Check.
