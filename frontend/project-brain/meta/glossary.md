# Glossary (Spanish UI terms)

- Pedidos: order log (all orders today, any channel).
- Pedidos de cocina (/cocina): kitchen board — Nuevos, En curso, Listos, Entregados y cobrados.
- Nuevos: orders received, not started.
- En curso: orders being prepared.
- Listos: orders ready to hand over.
- Entregados y cobrados: orders handed over and paid in the current shift (the history).
- Turno: the shift; opens at "Turno abierto · HH:mm".
- Retrasado: an unfinished order past its pickup time (red).
- Agotado: product with zero stock (sold out).
- Cierre: special closure of the bar (date + reason).
- Cerrar turno: end the shift (not built).
- Recoge: pickup time.
- Carta: menu and products.
- Consola en vivo: live event feed (/consola).
